Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our international shipping services.

How do international shipping rates work?

Rates depend on the route, service, package dimensions, actual or volumetric weight, and shipment contents. Send these details in a quote request and we’ll confirm the price and applicable charges before you book.

How can I track my shipment?

Enter the tracking number our team gives you after your shipment is registered. The page shows status events recorded by Trust Move; it does not show live GPS location. If you need an update, call us.

Do you offer door-to-door delivery?

Pickup and final delivery depend on your exact address and destination. Include the pickup area, destination city, and postal code in your quote request so we can confirm what is available.

Can you ship documents securely?

We handle document shipments. Ask us about packaging and handling for your specific item. Delivery timing depends on the route and service, so we’ll provide an estimate with your quote.

How do I get an accurate price quote?

Use the quote form and include the destination city and postal code, package dimensions and weight, contents, and preferred pickup date. We’ll review the route and contact you to confirm availability, charges, and an estimated delivery range. For urgent requests, call us.

How long will delivery take?

Timing varies by destination, service, handoffs, and customs processing. We’ll share an estimated delivery range with your quote; customs or carrier delays can affect it.

Are there items I cannot send?

Carrier rules and destination-country restrictions apply, and some items may need extra documents or may not be accepted. Tell us exactly what you plan to send and wait for confirmation before handing it over.

When will I hear back about my quote?

Our team will review your request and contact you using the email or phone number you provide. We don’t publish a fixed response time yet; for urgent shipments, call us directly.
